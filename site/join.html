Ride with us

A strong, team-oriented model — experienced riders passing down what they know, season after season.

The Hagens Berman cycling team is built on a strong team oriented model, something that is often missing from amateur-level competitive cycling today. More experienced riders share their knowledge with less experienced team members. In addition to racing, members are encouraged to be active in the club and assist in the club’s operations and projects.

We field men’s, women’s, masters, and development squads across all disciplines — whether you’re lining up for your first race or chasing upgrade points, there’s a place for you here.

Women’s program

We are actively growing our women’s program.

We’re recruiting women racers at every level and every discipline, and we participate in and support community women’s cycling events around the PNW.

If you’re curious about racing but not sure where to start, reach out — we’ll connect you with riders who have been exactly where you are.
