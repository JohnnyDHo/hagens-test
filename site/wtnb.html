01

The night

One evening, two parts: learn the ropes, then put them straight to work. No experience required — that’s kind of the whole point.

Skills clinic

6:00 PM

Learn the circle

Led by Craig Undem — founder of Cycle University and 40 years of racing and coaching. Free with race registration, held in the north parking lot. Cornering, pacelines, and everything you need to line up confident.

WTNB race

6:45 PM

Race the circle

A 30-minute, beginner-friendly race on the closed circuit — USAC points are on the table if you want them. Every other field runs as normally scheduled, so you get the full race-night atmosphere.

Free entries available — just ask.

02

What you need

The bar to entry is deliberately low. If you can check these four boxes, you can race Tuesday night.

  • 01 $10 Pre-reg entry — normally $30
  • 02 $15 One-day USAC license
  • 03 Any bike The one you already own works
  • 04 Helmet Required — always

That’s it — register →

03

Course & getting there

Two miles of wide, flat, fast closed circuit — no sharp corners, plenty of room to breathe.

The course is a two-mile closed circuit at Pacific Raceways: smooth asphalt, generous corners, and a start/finish in front of the grandstands. It’s about as friendly as a racecourse gets — which is exactly why we picked it.

Study up before Tuesday: Strava segment ↗

Drive

Pacific Raceways sits in Kent, WA — roughly an hour from Seattle outside of rush hour.

Carpool

Racing is better with friends in the passenger seat — sign up to share a ride or grab a spot with someone heading south.

Sounder + ride

Take the Sounder train to Auburn, then ride the rest of the way — a route is mapped on RideWithGPS.
