## Verified team truth

- Team name: **Hagens Berman Racing Seattle**.
- The team is based in the Seattle area and races throughout the Pacific Northwest.
- Core invitation: **Everyone can race.**
- The team has been established for **more than 20 years**.
- Disciplines: road, gravel, cyclocross, track, and mountain biking.
- The team is grounded in community and team racing: experienced riders share knowledge, racing is collective, and members give time back to the local cycling scene.
- Programs and support include weekly team rides, skills and race-strategy clinics, training camp, and race support.
- Team squads: men, women, masters, and development.
- Membership dues are **$75**.
- Membership requires **one team kit** and volunteering at **one local race**.
- Hagens Berman Racing Seattle is a **501(c)(3) nonprofit**.

## Prohibited or unverified claims

Do not publish any of the following without new written source approval:

- An exact founding year.
- Exact current roster or membership counts.
- “Winningest,” “most successful,” “best,” or similar unsupported superlatives.
- Giordana as a current partner or kit supplier.
- Invented race wins, results, podiums, records, statistics, or performance totals.
- Unverified rider names, bios, quotes, testimonials, alumni identities, or professional affiliations.
- Unverified prices, discounts, reimbursement amounts, registration availability, or event logistics.
- Claims that every ride or program is open to the public. The official site specifically describes weekly team rides as open to friendly riders; keep other access language precise.

## Editorial rules

- Prefer concrete, active language rooted in team behavior: ride together, share knowledge, race as a team, volunteer locally.
- “Seattle,” “Pacific Northwest,” and “more than 20 years” are safe anchors. Do not back-calculate a founding year.
- Photography may depict real racing atmosphere without identifying a rider unless identity is verified.
- Placeholder media must never be captioned as a specific team member, race result, or event moment.
- Before a factual section passes review, compare every number, proper name, partner, and date against this document and the live official source.
