## Home copy (canonical)
> Hagens Berman Racing Seattle is an amateur cycling race team competing in road, gravel, cyclocross, track, and mountain bike races throughout the Pacific Northwest. Our team has been established for over 20 years, and our alumni include riders who have gone on to race professionally. We seek to develop riders of all ages and abilities to race successfully while maintaining healthy balance with other aspects of life.

> **Racing happens as a community, not as an individual.** We don't only race hard, we give our time back. Hagens Berman hosts two road races in March, and our members regularly volunteer at other events throughout the year. We foster a welcoming, enthusiastic, and inclusive culture within the team - an attitude that we bring to races.

> We strive to win and have fun while fueling the development of riders and of cycling in the local community. To support that, we run weekly team rides (open to all friendly riders), organized skills and race-strategy clinics, and team training camps — and we back it up with real in-race support. **We race as a team**, not as a group of individuals wearing the same kit.

CTA on home: "GET IN TOUCH!" → mailto/contact. Home embeds the Instagram feed.

## About / club model (footer "info-content" block, appears site-wide)
> The Hagens Berman cycling team is built on a strong team oriented model, something that is often missing from amateur-level competitive cycling today. More experienced riders share their knowledge with less experienced team members. In addition to racing, members are encouraged to be active in the club and assist in the club's operations and projects.

Benefits listed there:
1. Organized group training rides and the opportunity to find training partners. A detailed annual training plan which can be tailored to a member's individual abilities and goals
2. Coaching in physical training, cycling skills, and racing tactics and strategy
3. Financial support in the form of equipment discounts from sponsors and reimbursement for selected race fees and travel
4. HB contributes to the sport by promoting two races each year.

## Event: WTNB Night at Pacific Raceways (/wtnb-night-at-pr)
- **Tues August 25, 2026 · Kent, WA** — 6:00 PM Skills Clinic, 6:45 PM WTNB Race; all other fields run as normally scheduled. Register: https://www.bikereg.com/pacific-raceways-circuit-series
- WTNB = Women, Trans, Non-Binary. All WTNB riders welcome, all skill levels, all bikes.
- Clinic led by **Craig Undem**, founder of Cycle University (2013–19), 40 years racing/coaching. Free w/ race reg, north parking lot.
- Race: 30-minute beginner-friendly race, USAC points available. $10 pre-reg (normally $30) + $15 one-day USAC license. Free entries available — ask.
- Course: 2-mile closed circuit at Pacific Raceways, wide/flat/fast, no sharp corners; Strava segment https://www.strava.com/segments/1597830
- What you need: any working bike, helmet required, $10 entry, $15 one-day license.
- Getting there: drive (~1 hr from Seattle), carpool sign-up, or Sounder to Auburn + ride (ridewithgps route).
- Presented by: Sticky Bidon, Seas To Trees Adventures, Formula 369, Hagens Berman.
- Tone of this page is warm/playful ("Bring a friend, bring the bike you already own, and let's go ride in circles together.")
